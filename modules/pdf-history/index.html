02 / Before the portable page

Send the
paper, then.

Paper kept the layout. A native file kept the ability to edit. Sending one didn’t give you all the advantages of the other.

Warnock’s Camelot proposal asked for a practical electronic route between applications and machines. Existing print technology was a starting point.

Moving the bytes wasn’t the same as moving the page.

RESEARCH NOTE 01 ↗

03 / The technical idea

Don’t send
the workshop.
Pack the page.

Let the authoring application settle the layout. Send a representation of the composed page that a separate reader can display. PDF preserves that chosen pagination; it does not undo an author’s already-made layout choices.

PDF combines positioned text, vector graphics and images. Embedded fonts can reduce dependence on the recipient’s font collection.

Not a screenshot. Not automatically self-contained, either: fonts can be left out, and files still need compatible readers. Missing resources or broken character mappings can still affect rendering or text extraction.

04 / The receiving problem

Portable
isn’t enough.

Acrobat arrived in 1993. Adoption was difficult: people still needed a way to open the files.

Reader became free with Acrobat 2.0 in 1994. In a later interview, Adobe’s team described pressure from competing free readers and web browsers.

A better file still needs somewhere to land.

This was a distribution decision as much as a software decision.

05 / Value creation & value capture

Free to read.
Valuable
to make.

A larger receiving audience makes a publishing tool more useful. Adobe could charge for creating and managing documents while distributing Reader for free.

For an organisation, electronic distribution offered a different workflow: share a file, then print only where paper is useful.

The business mechanism here is an interpretation—not a claim that free Reader alone caused PDF’s success.

06 / The familiar arrival

I know what
I’m receiving.

The page they sent.
The page I open.
The page I print.

A familiar vehicle for information: less negotiation about how to receive it. Something recognisable survives the journey.

This is Achim’s experience and design hypothesis, not a measured claim that everyone trusts PDFs. Successful page fidelity depends on the file, resources and receiving software.

Illustrative signature field

APPROVAL / SAMPLE ONLY

No real signature. No validation performed.

Important documents can arrive as PDFs, sometimes with signatures. But what kind of reassurance are we asking for?

Appearance, signatures and truth are different questions
Familiar format
Can I receive and inspect the document as intended?
Provenance
Where did this version come from, and who is associated with it?
Integrity
Has the relevant signed content changed?
Identity / authorship
Who is the asserted author or signer, and how is that identity established?
Truth / applicability
Is the information correct and relevant here?

A pasted or drawn signature image is not a cryptographic digital signature. Validation depends on the signing mechanism, certificates, trust settings, software and later changes. It does not establish that every statement is true. PDFs are not inherently signed, immutable or authentic. [5]

Appearance alone also cannot establish correct reading order, a current version or the surrounding conversation. [4]

02 / LLM-chat experience

An answer arrives.
Does experience return?

Contributors and disagreements can be harder to inspect behind a quick answer. Separately, a useful investigation with an agent can remain inside a private chat.

Sources → answer: context?Private finding → shared contribution?

Two gaps: context arriving with an answer, and new experience returning to shared knowledge.

Open Presence explores genuine human signals and inspectable provenance through the Photo Frame Commons pilot.

“Human” does not mean correct; “AI-assisted” does not mean fake. Keep authorship, declared AI involvement, evidence and interpretation distinguishable. No format proves humanness.

An invitation to an experiment, not a solved problem. ATProto remains a candidate portable record layer; it does not itself guarantee truth, human identity, retention, discovery, moderation or community.

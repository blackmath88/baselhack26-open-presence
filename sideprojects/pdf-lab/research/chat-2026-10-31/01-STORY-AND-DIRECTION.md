## Why it is compelling

A PDF presents itself as a stable page. Internally it is a graph of objects interpreted by several subsystems. Outside the file, services manufacture thumbnails, text, search indexes, OCR, review state and model inputs. The same bytes can have multiple readings; the same visible experience can depend on different bytes and external state.

The productive artistic move is to make the discrepancy intentional and legible:

- This picture is one letter: glyph drawing and text mapping tell different stories.
- This document has a backstage: an author-built secret interface unlocks another experience.
- This document remembers: earlier revisions become archaeology.
- I am two files: different parsers find different format identities.
- This document is its own workshop: source and construction history travel with the artefact.
- Please print me to change me: transformation becomes part of the composition.
- Every reader repairs me differently: a tiny broken file reveals recovery assumptions.
- The page is a world: navigation through pages acts as gameplay.

These are proposals or thematic descriptions of prior art, not universal PDF capabilities. See the source catalogue and actual local measurements.

## The five kinds of bending

1. Specification gymnastics: legal features arranged unexpectedly.
2. Compatibility limits: supported in one environment, absent or differently exposed in another.
3. Parser disagreement and repair: malformed or ambiguous inputs receive different interpretations.
4. Self-reference and multiple identity: source/output combinations, polyglots and hashquines.
5. Trust-boundary crossing: historical security failures and their prerequisites.

These categories overlap; they are not a formal taxonomy or escalating score. A polyglot is not necessarily parser disagreement; a collision demonstrates a hash property, not automatically a PDF flaw. A crash is not proof of code execution, and supported scripting is not sandbox escape.

Category five belongs in the reading room. Default live experiments remain owned, synthetic, bounded and harmless. Intentional secret entrances into an artwork are not unauthorised access or device backdoors.

## Connection to Adobe and agents

The useful historical analogy is an expectation that a document could survive transmission in a recognisable form. It does not mean PDF guaranteed truth, identical behaviour or complete preservation of meaning.

When information travels through agents, ask what actually survives: visible page, native text, logical order, provenance, uncertainty, revision identity, citations or external conversation? This is a proposal for an explicit survival contract, not a claim that such a protocol exists.

An agent hop can actively summarise and transform. A cloud review can keep state outside the file. Therefore bytes alone may not reproduce the experience. The more honest promise is to disclose the supplied representation, source revision, transformations and remaining unknowns.

## Editorial principles

- Start with an impossible-sounding artefact, then reveal the construction.
- Teach through prediction, exploration and dissection rather than alarm.
- Keep useful static fallbacks and accessibility in scope.
- Let a report demonstrate the feature it explains where practical.
- Keep screenshots, structure, extraction and interpretation separate.
- Preserve failures and unknowns; do not invent a success to keep the story smooth.
- Do not assert novelty merely because the combination feels new.
- Defer merging with XL LAB until both have demonstrated a concrete shared need.

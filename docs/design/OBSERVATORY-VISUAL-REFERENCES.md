## Design synthesis
Use stable workstreams, honest time, selected relationships and explicit resume context. Research and implementation can coexist. Findings can return without closing an exploration. These are proposed applications of the references, not experimentally validated claims about Observatory.

Five evaluation questions: What is the mission? What is happening now? What needs judgment? Why does this branch exist? Where do I resume?

## Rejected directions
Decorative images as the source of visual meaning; warm paper/cream canvas; a rigid research-to-build pipeline; an unrestricted global canvas as the default; inferred duration/progress from commits; treating side exploration as failure.

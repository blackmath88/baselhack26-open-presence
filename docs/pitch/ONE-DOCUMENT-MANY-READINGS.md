# One Document. Many Readings.
Proposed Adobe-facing research exhibition · 4 November 2026

**Opening question.** You and an AI receive the same PDF. Which reading would you quote?

## The 35-second opening for the PHF pitch

Hold on the page and read “I leave a lamp beside the rain.” Reveal the extracted line “I keep a tide beneath my tongue.” Keep the drawing still. Say “We wrote both lines. Which poem would you quote?” Then return to PHF: “When someone quotes you through an AI, what context would you want to travel with that answer?”

Use the [existing opening](../../sideprojects/pdf-lab/opening.html) and recorded extraction until copy/paste is qualified on the presentation viewer. PHF retains the main part of the [three-minute film](PHF-THREE-MINUTE-VIDEO.md).
